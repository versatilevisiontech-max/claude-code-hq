# Fashion caption

- Name the garment, the cloth, and how it sits.
- Credit cut, seam, and drape. Skip lifestyle slogans.
- Instagram: one or two sentences.
- LinkedIn: one short paragraph on making.
- X: one sentence.
- YouTube: a hook line, then 20–35 seconds of voiceover.
